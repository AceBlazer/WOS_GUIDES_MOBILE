Privacy Policy

How the WOS Guides mobile app handles information.

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how WOS Guides (the “App”) handles information when you use it. The App provides game guides and tools, including an optional Android auto-clicker. Please read this policy before using the App.

Information the App handles

  • Guide requests and technical data: The App contacts its configured guide service to retrieve categories and guide content. The service and network providers may receive connection information such as your IP address, request time, and technical request details as part of delivering and securing the service. The service operator’s actual logging and retention settings depend on its server configuration.
  • On-device settings and cache: The App saves whether onboarding has been completed and may cache guide data on your device to support app performance and offline behavior. This information is stored locally and is not a user account.
  • Push notifications (optional): After onboarding, the App asks for permission to send notifications. If you allow them, OneSignal and the relevant push-notification platform process a push subscription identifier and associated technical/device information to deliver notifications. You can turn notifications off in your device settings. Their handling is also subject to the providers’ privacy notices.
  • Android auto-clicker permissions (optional): If you choose to use the auto-clicker, Android asks you to enable its Accessibility Service and, where applicable, display-over-other-apps access. The service uses accessibility gestures to perform taps at the position you select. The App’s current implementation does not inspect screen content or send accessibility content to our servers. These permissions can be revoked in Android settings.

The App does not currently provide account registration or include an advertising SDK. We do not use information handled by the App to build advertising profiles.

How information is used

Information is used to provide guide content, remember basic app state, deliver notifications when enabled, operate the auto-clicker when you request it, and maintain the security and reliability of the App and its services.

Sharing and service providers

Guide-service infrastructure, OneSignal, and Apple or Google push-notification services may process the information needed for their functions. They act under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the App, its users, or others.

Retention and deletion

On-device onboarding data and cached content can generally be removed by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling the App. Push subscription data is handled through OneSignal and the platform notification services; turning notifications off stops future notifications but may not immediately erase provider records. Server-side request logs, if kept, are retained according to the guide-service operator’s configuration and applicable requirements. Contact us to ask about a privacy request.

Children’s privacy

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.

Your choices and rights

You can decline or revoke notification and Android auto-clicker permissions through your device settings, and you can remove locally stored App data by clearing storage or uninstalling. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights concerning personal information. Contact us to make a request.

International users and security

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. We use reasonable measures appropriate to the services we operate, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or its data practices change. The effective date above indicates when the current version took effect. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
